What it does.

Three functions in one reusable product.

  • Odor

    Designed to help manage the odors that build up in shoes through repeated use.

  • Moisture

    Designed to help address dampness from sweat and everyday activity.

  • Fragrance

    A fragrance component intended to leave shoes smelling fresher between wears.

Reusable. Built for repeated use, not thrown away.

Questions.

What is SoleFresh?

A reusable shoe-care product being developed to help manage shoe odor, moisture and freshness.

Who is it for?

Organizations that manage shared or high-use footwear, plus individual athletes and everyday wearers.

How do you use it?

Place it inside your shoes when they aren't being worn. Remove it before wearing them again.

Is it reusable?

Yes. Lifespan and care instructions are still being determined.

Does it have a fragrance?

Yes. Specific scents and the fragrance system haven't been announced.

How long does it take to work?

Performance testing is still needed before we can give a reliable estimate.

How much does it cost?

Final pricing hasn't been set, and there's nothing to buy yet. Affordability is a development priority. Facilities and teams can join the pilot to help test it.

When will it be available?

It's in development. No launch date has been announced.
