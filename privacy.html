Privacy

SoleFresh is a student-founded startup developed through the INCubatoredu program. This page covers what you send us through the forms on this site.

What we collect

The facility pilot form asks for your organization's name and type, your name and email, and optionally the approximate pairs of shoes you manage, a phone number and a message. The waitlist form asks for your email.

How we use it

We'll only use this to contact you about SoleFresh.

Where it goes

When you submit a form, this site's server passes your details to Google Sheets, a Google service. They are saved as a row in a private spreadsheet in a Google account we control, on Google's servers. We keep them for as long as we need them to contact you about SoleFresh, and delete them when you ask. See Google’s privacy policy.

This site

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It remembers the audience you picked in your browser for the current visit, so the pilot form can fill in your organization type. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address. To stop repeat submissions, the server briefly holds your IP address in memory. The site is hosted on Vercel.
